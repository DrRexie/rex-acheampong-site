My story

From a math classroom in Ghana to Ghana's digital health future

I started as a mathematics teacher, and teaching still shapes how I work. I want complex data to be clear enough for the people who have to act on it. My career has taken me across three countries and back to where it began.

  1. 1990 – 1993Cape Coast, Ghana

    Trained to teach

    Associate degree and certificate in Mathematics Education at the University of Cape Coast.

  2. 1998 – 2002London, UK

    Learned the systems

    IT Support Specialist for the London Borough of Lewisham, managing secure access and supporting public-sector users.

  3. 2003 – 2023New Jersey, USA

    Built the expertise

    Network administration, then a BS in Mathematics, followed by 13 years in Rutgers clinical and research settings while earning an MS and a PhD in Biomedical Informatics.

  4. 2024 – todayNew Jersey & Accra

    Bringing it home

    As Chief Informatics Officer at Strategic eHealth Solutions, I lead informatics and AI strategy for Ghana's digital health transformation.
